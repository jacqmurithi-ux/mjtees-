# MJ Tees — project overview

## Purpose

Build one connected system for MJ Tees: a public website for customers and a private operations application for the shop team.

## Business profile

- One physical shop in Kenya, with 2–5 staff members and owner oversight.
- Walk-in/POS sales and made-to-order customised jobs.
- In-house screen printing, artwork preparation, sublimation and vinyl work.
- DTF sticker printing is outsourced; other outsourcing may be recorded per job.
- Customers collect at the shop or arrange delivery.

## Products and services

- Blank and branded T-shirts, polos, caps and other apparel.
- Schoolwear, uniforms and bulk group orders.
- Screen printing, sublimation, vinyl branding and heat-applied customisation.
- Outsourced DTF stickers and custom decals.
- Vehicle/motorcycle graphics.
- Mugs, reflector jackets and other custom merchandise.
- Artwork preparation and print-ready file production.

## Operating rules already established

- Keep selected blank merchandise and consumables in stock; purchase shortages after an order is received.
- Track stock by product variant such as size and colour.
- A quote is job-specific and depends on quantity, material, complexity, method, labour, outsourced work and margin.
- Deposits vary by order. Record the amount required and every payment received.
- Full payment is required before collection or delivery unless an owner-approved exception is explicitly recorded.
- Current payment methods are M-Pesa Paybill and cash. Automatic payment-provider integration is not part of this foundation.
- Cash deposits and Paybill settlement transfers into the central business account are transfers, not additional sales revenue.
- Preserve the history of quotes, prices, payments, stock movements and approvals.

## First release objective

A working first release covering core operations, not just a point-of-sale screen:

1. Owner and staff accounts and permissions.
2. Public business website and customer enquiries.
3. Customers and contact history.
4. Products, variants, stock and stock reservations.
5. Walk-in sales, quotations and custom orders.
6. Suppliers, purchasing and goods receiving.
7. Deposits, instalments, balances, receipts and reconciliation.
8. Production jobs, tasks, artwork approvals and outsourced work.
9. Expenses, job costing and daily close.
10. Collection/delivery controls and owner reporting.

## Information quality

Existing records are spread across notebooks, WhatsApp, invoices and payment records. During migration, verified transactions must be distinguished from estimates or incomplete historical information. Never silently convert an estimate into a confirmed transaction.
